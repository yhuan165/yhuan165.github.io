My lab recently leveraged 1000 genomes project samples to generate RNA sequencing datasets from 731 individuals from 26 globally-diverse populations (MAGE). I will leverage this data to better understand genetic variation in human populations. Historically, the data generated within the scientific enterprise have focused on individuals of European ancestry, resulting in datasets of limited utility, even for studying genetics in those populations. With the increase in computational power and tools, I aim to contribute to increasing diversity in genomics and develop tools that facilitate further discoveries.

Trans-eQTLs have been difficult to study as the increase in number of tests due to the larger frame of interactions exponentially increases the computational burden. I aim to develop a method to look at trans-eQTL or borrow newly created tools to test for trans-eQTLs in the MAGE data.
