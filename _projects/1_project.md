Dosage compensation is heavily debated in the field. Dosage compensation occurs when increased copies of a gene does not result in a linear increase of gene expression as seen in RNA-seq. 

Recent papers have demonstrated that previous findings of genes undergoing dosage compensation in trisomy 21 was due to incorrect null hypothesis setting in the statistical methods. I hope to test this in Cancer data.
