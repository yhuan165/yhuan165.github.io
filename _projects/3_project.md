We hypothesized that survival of aneuploid embryos to blastocyst stage may depend on the sex of the embryos. The observed excess of male (XY) versus female (XX) euploid embryos (51.5% XY, p = 1.32 x 10-19) was significantly stronger among embryos affected by aneuploidy of a single autosome (52.8% XY, p = 1.43 x 10-4). Chromosomes 15 (55.1% XY, p = 5.76 x 10-4) and 16 (54.2% XY, p = 1.79 x 10-4) exhibited individually significant male biases.
